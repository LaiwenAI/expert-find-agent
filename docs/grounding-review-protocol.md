# Independent grounding review

Review each record without consulting model predictions. The local review package contains private source text and is not a public dataset. Each reviewer should work on a separate copy and use a stable, non-identifying reviewer ID. Record an explanation for every judgment, especially uncertainty or disagreement.

For each language view, determine whether the selected request is supported by the original need and cited segments. A derived request may omit unrelated obligations for its stated intent, but must not alter a condition attached to its selected operation or introduce an unsupported object, threshold, capability, actor, or completed achievement. The source can request a solution without demonstrating that a solution exists. Inspect the full original need for scope and attribution; cited segments alone may omit context.

For bilingual equivalence, compare the two derived requests on their selected operation, object, conditions, modality, negation, units and thresholds. Fluency is not sufficient. A pair can be equivalent while both versions add an unsupported condition, so equivalence and grounding are separate judgments.

Use `supported` when the relevant relationship is established, `changed_or_unsupported` when a substantive contradiction or addition is evident, and `uncertain` when available text is insufficient or ambiguous. Do not use missing evidence to infer an expert's inability. Fill `chinese_grounding`, `english_grounding`, and `bilingual_equivalence` independently, and include a rationale and reviewer ID. Leave case identifiers and source text unchanged.

Two independent reviews per case are desirable. Preserve each review separately, report raw agreement and the full confusion matrix including uncertainty, and resolve disagreements only in a separate adjudication record. Record missing reviews and do not convert them to negative labels. These 30 selected diagnostic cases are not a random sample of all enterprise needs; their agreement or accuracy cannot be generalized without a separate sampling design.

The current export has six cases for each of the five intent types. It was selected from the earlier overlap-stratified diagnostic rather than sampled uniformly. Validate each completed copy with `python -m experiments.bilingual.check_grounding_review PATH`, keeping the original blind export and its manifest unchanged. The validator checks the frozen source fields and counts missing judgments but does not assess the correctness of a review.

## Stratified construction-set review

`experiments.bilingual.export_stratified_review` prepares a separate local packet with ten uniformly sampled pairs in each intent-type and screening-status stratum: 100 pairs total. The review forms omit screening status, query IDs and parent IDs; do not give reviewers `private-mapping.json` or the screen results. The full original need is provided for grounding; no cited-segment field is supplied in this packet. Reviewers may see the intent type. Text may itself reveal information about a source, so this is not full source-identity blinding.

Use the same three labels and separate reviewer copies as above. Validate a completed copy with `python -m experiments.bilingual.check_grounding_review PATH --root artifacts/stratified-grounding-human-review-v1`. The original packet currently has no human judgments. The sample deliberately oversamples unjudged strata: use population stratum sizes from the local manifest when estimating construction-set rates, report missing and uncertain responses explicitly, and account for repeated parent queries in uncertainty analysis. It cannot support claims about protected splits or expert relevance. The original packet predates the addition of generator-code and private-mapping hashes to the exporter; do not claim that its manifest records these hashes.

For one review copy, `python -m experiments.bilingual.analyze_stratified_review PATH` validates the sample mapping against the deterministic selection and frozen dataset, then reports a population-weighted sample interval with missing and uncertain judgments unresolved. It also reports conservative finite-population bounds that leave every unsampled pair unknown. Neither is a confidence interval; sampling variation and reviewer disagreement need separate analysis. With the current blank forms both intervals are [0, 1].

## Complete-original translation review

`experiments.bilingual.export_translation_review` prepares a separate local packet of the 78 complete outputs generated in the three 32-parent development pilots. Forms hide method and model-screen outcomes and show the complete source need and complete English translation. The same source appears in multiple cases and its text can reveal identity; this is not full source or parent blinding. The 18 failed or incomplete generations are absent from review forms but must remain in the original 32-attempt-per-arm denominator.

Review each translation independently for full coverage of obligations, actors, objects, numerical quantities, units, comparison direction, negation, modality and uncertainty. Use `faithful_complete`, `changed_or_incomplete`, or `uncertain`, with a reason and non-identifying reviewer ID. Two independent reviewers should use separate copies; adjudicate disagreements separately. Validate a copy with `python -m experiments.bilingual.check_translation_review ROOT REVIEW_COPY`. All 78 forms are currently blank. The dataset is private, and neither model-screen outcomes nor future human review alone confer publication rights.

`experiments.bilingual.export_segmented_review` separately prepares 31 completed translations from the matched 16-parent direct/segmented pilot, using the same fields and validator. One direct generation is absent from the packet and remains an unresolved attempt in the trial denominator. Its 31 labels are also all missing; repeated source needs and some direct translations overlap the earlier review packet, so the packets cannot be treated as independent samples.

The private `enterprise-adaptive-human-review-v1` packet has 31 returned result cases from four development needs, with three review dimensions: factual support, query relevance, and personal-role support. Use `python -m experiments.retrieval.check_enterprise_adaptive_review <packet-directory> <review-jsonl>` to verify the immutable blinded content and mapping, validate labels and reviewer notes, and calculate worst-case bounds for missing or uncertain judgments. The blank packet has bounds of [0, 1] for each arm and dimension. These bounds concern returned cases only: one attempt is unresolved, experts may recur, and candidate sets differ. The packet and completed reviews contain private source text and remain local.
